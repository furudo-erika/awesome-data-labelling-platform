## Why Data Labelling Platform Matters

The right data labelling platform speeds up annotators, enforces quality, and keeps datasets organized and versioned. The wrong one creates exports nobody can trust. Modern platforms also add AI-assisted labelling, data curation, and model evaluation in one place.

## Selection Criteria

Every entry in this list of best data labelling platform was assessed against these criteria:

- Annotation editors for your data types
- AI-assisted labelling and model-in-the-loop
- Review workflows, consensus, and quality metrics
- APIs, SDKs, and cloud storage integrations
- Deployment options: SaaS, VPC, or on-prem

## Top 10 Data Labelling Platform Reviewed

### 1. Labelbox

[Labelbox](https://labelbox.com/) — Labelbox offers a data-labeling and curation platform plus the Alignerr network of expert labelers for RLHF, evaluations, and multimodal annotation.

- **Type:** Labeling platform + expert workforce
- **Best for:** Teams that want software and an on-demand expert labeling network in one place
- **Consider:** Platform costs plus workforce costs; compare against bring-your-own-labeler setups.

### 2. SuperAnnotate

[SuperAnnotate](https://www.superannotate.com/) — SuperAnnotate is a data-labeling and evaluation platform with customizable editors for text, image, video, and LLM tasks, plus an optional managed workforce marketplace.

- **Type:** Labeling platform
- **Best for:** Custom LLM and multimodal annotation workflows with built-in QA
- **Consider:** Highly configurable; plan time for workflow setup.

### 3. Encord

[Encord](https://encord.com/) — Encord combines annotation, data curation, and model evaluation for image, video, medical imaging, audio, and documents, with micro-model assisted labeling.

- **Type:** Multimodal data platform
- **Best for:** Computer vision, video, DICOM, and multimodal data curation and labeling
- **Consider:** Strongest for vision and medical data; check text tooling depth for your use case.

### 4. V7 Darwin

[V7 Darwin](https://www.v7labs.com/) — V7 Darwin offers AI-assisted annotation (auto-annotate, SAM-based segmentation), dataset management, and workflow automation, with strong support for medical imaging.

- **Type:** Labeling platform
- **Best for:** Fast AI-assisted image, video, and medical labeling
- **Consider:** Per-seat and usage pricing; compare at your expected volume.

### 5. Scale AI

[Scale AI](https://scale.com/) — Scale AI runs one of the largest human-data operations in the industry, covering LLM post-training data, model evaluations, red teaming, and image, video, and LiDAR annotation through its Data Engine.

- **Type:** Managed data engine
- **Best for:** Frontier labs and enterprises that need large volumes of RLHF, evaluation, and computer-vision data
- **Consider:** Enterprise-oriented engagement model; pricing is quoted per project.

### 6. Dataloop

[Dataloop](https://dataloop.ai/) — Dataloop is an AI development platform that combines data management, annotation, pipelines, and a marketplace of models and labeling services.

- **Type:** Data management + labeling
- **Best for:** End-to-end unstructured data pipelines with labeling built in
- **Consider:** Broad platform; may be more than you need for simple labeling.

### 7. Kili Technology

[Kili Technology](https://kili-technology.com/) — Kili Technology provides collaborative labeling for images, text, documents, and video, with quality workflows, LLM evaluation projects, and on-prem deployment options.

- **Type:** Labeling platform
- **Best for:** European teams that need text, image, and LLM-evaluation labeling with strong QA
- **Consider:** Confirm current product focus and deployment model during evaluation.

### 8. Amazon SageMaker Ground Truth

[Amazon SageMaker Ground Truth](https://aws.amazon.com/sagemaker/groundtruth/) — SageMaker Ground Truth lets you build labeling workflows with private workforces, vendors from AWS Marketplace, or Mechanical Turk, and Ground Truth Plus offers a fully managed option.

- **Type:** Cloud labeling service
- **Best for:** AWS-native teams that want labeling inside their existing cloud account
- **Consider:** Tied to AWS; UI is functional rather than polished.

### 9. Label Studio

[Label Studio](https://labelstud.io/) — Label Studio by HumanSignal is the most popular open-source data labeling tool, supporting text, image, audio, video, and time series, with ML-backend integration and an Enterprise edition.

- **Type:** Open-source labeling software
- **Best for:** Self-hosted, multi-modal labeling with an enterprise upgrade path
- **Consider:** Self-hosting means you own uptime, security, and upgrades.

### 10. Roboflow

[Roboflow](https://roboflow.com/) — Roboflow Annotate offers AI-assisted labeling (including SAM-powered tools) inside a platform that also handles dataset versioning, training, and deployment.

- **Type:** CV platform with labeling
- **Best for:** Computer-vision teams that want labeling, training, and deployment together
- **Consider:** Best value if you use the wider Roboflow stack.

## How to Choose the Best Data Labelling Platform

| If you need... | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| All-round platform plus workforce | Labelbox or SuperAnnotate |
| Vision, video, and medical imaging | Encord or V7 Darwin |
| Inside AWS | Amazon SageMaker Ground Truth |
| Open-source with enterprise option | Label Studio |
| Computer vision end to end | Roboflow |

Shortlist two or three options from this list, run a small paid pilot on your own data, and compare quality, turnaround, and total cost before signing a long-term contract.

## FAQ

### What is a data labelling platform?

Software for creating, reviewing, and managing labelled datasets, usually with annotation editors, workflow management, quality control, and export or API access.

### What is the best data labelling platform?

It depends on your data. Labelbox and SuperAnnotate are strong all-rounders; Encord and V7 Darwin lead for vision and medical data; Label Studio is the top open-source choice.

### Is there a free data labelling platform?

Label Studio Community and CVAT are free and open source. Most commercial platforms offer free tiers or trials.

### Platform or managed service?

Use a platform if you have annotators (in-house or a vendor). Use a managed service if you want finished labels delivered.
